IncubatorOS

Singapore Startup Grant & Institutional Venture Support Dashboard

IncubatorOS is a mock programme operations dashboard designed for Singapore-based startup support teams. It helps institutions track founder applications, startup grant readiness, research-commercialisation pathways, mentor matching, milestone evidence, pilot opportunities, and cohort-level outcomes.

Employer Case Study

Why this exists

IncubatorOS exists to model how an education-linked incubation team could keep founder intake, grant readiness, mentor coordination, milestone evidence, pilot opportunities, and reporting in one operating view.

Who it is for

Incubation managers, university venture teams, research commercialisation teams, public-sector innovation teams, accelerator operators, and founder support teams.

What it demonstrates

Intake design, cohort operations, startup screening, scoring logic, grant readiness, founder evidence tracking, mentor matching, milestone tracking, and stakeholder reporting.

What is real vs demo

This is a self-directed portfolio prototype. Metrics, startup records, founder profiles, and scoring outputs shown are sample data unless otherwise stated.

How this relates to my target roles

The project shows how I would structure programme rhythm for an incubator, university venture office, grant support team, or public innovation programme that needs clearer operating visibility.

Problem

Singapore startup support teams manage many founder pathways at once.

Institutional teams often support student founders, alumni startups, research teams, early-stage founders, grant applicants, incubation cohorts, mentors, advisors, and corporate or institutional pilot partners across overlapping workflows.

01

Scattered founder and grant data

Founder and grant data are often spread across forms, spreadsheets, emails, mentor notes, interview records, and programme trackers.

02

Limited readiness visibility

Institutions need clearer visibility into eligibility, milestones, evidence quality, funding readiness, and pathway fit.

03

Reporting takes extra effort

Programme teams need better reporting for grants, leadership, partners, and ecosystem outcomes without rebuilding dashboards every cycle.

Solution

Grant readiness, founder support, venture workflows, and KPIs in one dashboard.

IncubatorOS gives programme managers a structured view of founder pipelines, grant pathway fit, milestone evidence, mentor matching, pilot readiness, and stakeholder reporting.

01

Founder Pipeline Tracker

Track student, alumni, research, first-time founder, and spin-off teams from intake to programme support.

02

Grant Readiness Scoring

Score founder eligibility, validation, use of funds, milestones, team capability, and ecosystem fit.

03

Institution Pathway Recommender

Suggest suitable Singapore startup grant, university, research-commercialisation, or incubation pathways.

04

Mentor Matching Tracker

Match teams with mentors based on technology, market, grant, pilot, go-to-market, and fundraising needs.

05

Grant Milestone & Evidence Tracker

Monitor evidence submitted, use-of-funds plans, milestone completion, and readiness for next support steps.

06

Pilot Readiness Tracker

Assess customer discovery, pilot scope, technical readiness, institutional fit, and partner introduction needs.

07

Demo Day Timeline

Coordinate pitch reviews, demo rehearsals, founder updates, partner invitations, and investor Q&A preparation.

08

Founder Check-in Template

Use a repeatable support cadence for progress, blockers, evidence, mentor needs, and next measurable actions.

09

KPI & Stakeholder Reporting Dashboard

Summarise applications, teams supported, grant reviews, milestones, mentors, pilots, and demo day outcomes.

1. IntakeCapture founder profile, institution link, startup stage, sector, and support need.
2. Pathway reviewCompare grant, university, research-commercialisation, and incubator fit.
3. Evidence planDefine milestone proof, use-of-funds logic, prototype needs, and validation gaps.
4. Support matchingAssign mentors, partner introductions, pilot preparation, and founder check-ins.
5. ReportingTrack outcomes for leadership, grant administrators, partners, and ecosystem stakeholders.

CriteriaPointsWhat the review checks

Validation evidence15Customer interviews, pilots, LOIs, early usage, research validation, or market signals.

Singapore Ecosystem Fit

Relevance to Singapore Startup & Education Ecosystems

IncubatorOS is designed around the operating needs of Singapore's startup support ecosystem, where universities, research institutes, incubators, accelerators, and public-sector-linked programmes help founders move from idea to validation, funding readiness, pilots, and venture formation.

  • Supports startup grant readiness across founder, technology, accelerator, and equity-style pathways.
  • Helps institutions manage student, alumni, researcher, and early-stage founder pipelines.
  • Creates structure for mentor matching, milestone evidence, and founder check-ins.
  • Supports research-commercialisation and university spin-off workflows.
  • Enables clearer reporting for programme teams, leadership, funders, and ecosystem partners.
  • Helps founders prepare for grants, pilots, demo days, and investor conversations.

Portfolio Demonstration Notice

This project is a mock portfolio case study and is not an official grant application tool. Grant names, eligibility criteria, appointed partners, application windows, claim conditions, and funding amounts should always be verified against the latest official programme pages before use.

Closing Statement

IncubatorOS was designed as a practical portfolio project to demonstrate how structured programme operations can improve founder experience, grant readiness, institutional visibility, and venture-building outcomes.
