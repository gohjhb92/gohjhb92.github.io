Problem

Incubation teams often run on scattered information.

Founder applications, grant reviews, mentor assignments, milestone reporting, demo day logistics, and stakeholder updates can quickly spread across spreadsheets, emails, decks, chat threads, and static documents.

01

Fragmented founder and startup data

Programme teams need one reliable view of applicants, founders, startup maturity, sector focus, and evaluation notes.

02

Manual grant and milestone tracking

Grant approvals, milestone evidence, payout readiness, and reporting obligations become hard to monitor consistently.

03

Limited visibility into cohort KPIs

Leadership, funders, and partners need timely signals on founder progress, mentor engagement, pilots, and outcomes.

Solution

One structured dashboard for core incubation workflows.

IncubatorOS brings founder intake, evaluation, support, grant tracking, mentor coordination, KPI reporting, and demo day preparation into a single practical operating framework.

01

Founder Pipeline Tracker

Track status from application review to interview, shortlist, onboarding, and active cohort support.

02

Application Scoring Rubric

Score founders consistently across market, feasibility, founder capability, pilot potential, and strategic fit.

03

Mentor Matching Tracker

Match founders with sector, technical, go-to-market, fundraising, regulatory, and pilot access mentors.

04

KPI Dashboard

Monitor cohort health through applications, shortlist rates, mentor coverage, grants, pilots, and readiness.

05

Grant Milestone Tracker

Manage grant amounts, evidence collection, milestone completion, and payout readiness.

06

Demo Day Timeline

Coordinate founder onboarding, validation reviews, pitch refinement, rehearsal, and investor preparation.

07

Founder Check-in Template

Use a repeatable weekly check-in structure for progress, blockers, pilots, evidence, and next actions.

Application Scoring

Evaluation rubric for a 100-point founder application score.

Total score: 100
CriteriaWeightExplanation
Problem clarity12How clearly the startup defines the user pain, urgency, and current alternatives.
Market opportunity14Size, timing, customer segment focus, and relevance to an investable or scalable opportunity.
Product feasibility12Evidence that the proposed product can be built, tested, and improved during the cohort.
Founder capability14Team commitment, domain knowledge, execution capacity, and openness to feedback.
Business model10Revenue logic, pricing assumptions, customer willingness to pay, and go-to-market clarity.
Strategic fit with AI / digital finance14Alignment with AI, fintech, digital finance, responsible innovation, and institutional priorities.
Pilot potential12Likelihood of securing a meaningful customer, research, ecosystem, or industry pilot.
Grant milestone clarity12Quality of proposed milestones, evidence, budget use, and reporting readiness.

Relevance to AIDF

Why this fits a university-level digital finance institute.

IncubatorOS is shaped around the operating needs of institute-backed venture programmes that translate research, AI ideas, and digital finance concepts into founder pathways.

  • Supports venture pipeline development from application intake to cohort formation.
  • Helps translate AI and digital finance ideas into structured startup pathways.
  • Creates structure for founder support, mentor matching, and ecosystem engagement.
  • Enables reporting for grants, stakeholders, programme outcomes, and institutional visibility.
  • Supports demo days, pilot matchmaking, and ecosystem partnerships across universities, industry, and investors.
