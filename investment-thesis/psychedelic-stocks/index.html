This is my personal research note, not financial advice.

Psychedelic medicine stocks are not normal value stocks.

The main drivers are not current earnings, dividends, or near-term product revenue. They are clinical readouts, FDA interactions, trial design, financing decisions, and investor appetite for high-risk biotech stories.

The three names I am comparing here are Definium Therapeutics, GH Research, and AtaiBeckley.

My thesis is simple:

ATAI looks like the best risk-adjusted setup, DFTX is the cleanest near-term catalyst trade, and GHRS is high-quality science that I would rather buy on weakness.

My Ranking

Rank Stock My read
1 ATAI Best balance of upside, diversification, and runway.
2 DFTX Best pure catalyst trade, but the most binary.
3 GHRS Strong science and workflow story, but less obviously cheap after its rerating and financing.

ATAI is my preferred speculative core because it has more than one way to win. BPL-003 gives it a Phase 3-ready lead program, VLS-01 gives it another major 2026 catalyst, EMP-01 gives it early optionality in social anxiety, and management has guided runway into 2029.

DFTX is the most exciting if the goal is a fast trade around clinical catalysts. It has three Phase 3 DT120 ODT readouts expected in 2026, plus another MDD Phase 3 study now dosing. That is powerful, but it also makes the stock extremely event-driven.

GHRS may have one of the cleanest clinical workflow stories in the group. Its GH001 data in treatment-resistant depression are impressive, and the short psychoactive duration could matter commercially. The issue is that Phase 3 has not started yet, while the stock has already rerated and the company has added financing capacity.

The short version: all three have enough capital to keep developing their pipelines, but none of them should be treated like a cash-flow business. These are clinical-stage biotech trades.

DFTX

Definium Therapeutics, formerly MindMed, is a late-stage psychiatry biotech built around DT120 ODT, an oral lysergide D-tartrate formulation. Its main targets are generalized anxiety disorder, major depressive disorder, and PTSD. The company is also developing DT402 in autism spectrum disorder.

The bullish case is timing.

DT120 is already in multiple Phase 3 studies, and the company has guided to several key readouts in 2026:

Catalyst Expected timing
Emerge topline readout Late Q2 2026
Voyage topline readout Early Q3 2026
Panorama topline readout Late Q3 2026
Haven PTSD Phase 3 start Planned for 2027

That is a rare amount of high-impact data for one biotech stock in one year.

The problem is valuation and binary risk. DFTX already carries a large market cap relative to the rest of the group. If the readouts are clean, the stock can still rerate higher. But if the data are mixed, investors may quickly reprice the company toward cash plus limited pipeline option value.

My read: DFTX is the best instrument for a catalyst trade, not the easiest long-term core holding.

GHRS

GH Research is the most focused single-asset story in the group. Its lead program is GH001, an inhaled mebufotenin candidate for treatment-resistant depression. It also has GH002, an intravenous mebufotenin program, behind it.

The strongest part of the GHRS story is workflow.

In Phase 2b, GHRS reported a placebo-adjusted MADRS improvement of 15.5 points on Day 8, with 57.5% remission at Day 8 in the double-blind portion and 73% remission at six months in the open-label extension. The company also reported a median psychoactive duration of about 11 minutes, 99% discharge readiness within one hour, and no psychotherapy requirement in the study design.

If those features hold up in pivotal trials, GH001 could be much easier for clinics to deliver than longer-session psychedelic models.

The catch is timing. GHRS has not yet started Phase 3. The FDA lifted the GH001 clinical hold in January 2026, and the company is seeking alignment for a late-2026 global pivotal start. That is progress, but it still leaves investors waiting for the next real de-risking step.

The financing backdrop also matters. GHRS raised capital in April 2026 and then established a $200M ATM facility in May. That improves flexibility, but it can also cap upside if the company sells stock into strength.

My read: GHRS is high quality, but at the current setup I would rather buy pullbacks than chase.

ATAI

AtaiBeckley is the most diversified of the three.

After the combination of atai Life Sciences and Beckley Psytech, ATAI is more focused around rapid-acting mental health programs. The lead asset is BPL-003, an intranasal mebufotenin benzoate candidate for treatment-resistant depression.

ATAI has three main upside levers:

Program Why it matters
BPL-003 Phase 3-ready lead asset in treatment-resistant depression, with Breakthrough Therapy Designation.
VLS-01 DMT buccal film with Phase 2 Elumina topline results expected in Q4 2026.
EMP-01 Early social anxiety program with positive Phase 2a proof-of-concept signals.

BPL-003 is the centerpiece. ATAI reported a successful end-of-Phase 2 meeting with the FDA and said Phase 3 initiation remained on track for Q2 2026. The planned ReConnection pivotal program gives the company a clear late-stage path.

VLS-01 is the second important catalyst. It is designed around an approximately two-hour interventional psychiatry treatment paradigm, which could make it more practical than longer-duration psychedelic treatments if efficacy holds up.

EMP-01 is earlier, but it gives the stock another source of optionality. The Phase 2a social anxiety trial showed a placebo-adjusted LSAS improvement, patient-reported improvement, behavioral avoidance improvement, and no serious adverse events.

My read: ATAI is the best overall speculative setup because it does not depend on only one readout.

ATAI is not the cleanest binary catalyst. That is the point. It has several shots on goal, which makes it more attractive as a speculative position.

Main Risks

The biggest risk is clinical failure. These companies can lose large amounts of market value on one trial result, one safety signal, or one FDA interaction.

The second risk is regulatory friction. Psychedelic and Schedule I-related products can face extra commercialization steps even after clinical success. FDA alignment is not the same as approval.

The third risk is dilution. GHRS has the clearest near-term dilution overhang because of the ATM facility after its April financing. ATAI also has an ATM, though the source report noted no Q1 2026 sales under it. DFTX appears less urgent from a financing perspective because it has guided runway into 2028.

The fourth risk is sector sentiment. Psychedelic stocks can trade on narrative, social media enthusiasm, peer readouts, and broad biotech risk appetite. A good company-specific thesis can still get hit if the whole group sells off.

How I Would Trade It

Stock How I would treat it Entry approach Risk control
DFTX Catalyst trade first, long-term investment second. Start small on weakness rather than buying a full position into strength. Size for the possibility of a 30% to 50% clinical-data gap.
GHRS Pullback buy. Wait for retracements or clearer Phase 3 design and start timing. Respect the ATM overhang and trim if the stock runs hard into updates.
ATAI Core speculative position among the three. Scale in over time ahead of BPL-003 Phase 3 initiation and VLS-01 data. Use a thesis stop: reduce if VLS-01 disappoints and BPL-003 execution slips.

For a retail portfolio, I would keep sizing conservative. My generic rule for binary biotech is to keep each initial position small enough that a bad readout does not damage the whole portfolio. For many traders, that means something like 0.5% to 1.5% per name, with a strict cap on total exposure to the psychedelic biotech basket.

My Bottom Line

If I had to reduce this to one sentence:

Buy ATAI for the best overall upside setup, trade DFTX for the biggest near-term catalyst convexity, and wait for a better entry in GHRS unless you specifically want the ultra-short workflow bet.

ATAI is the name I would be most comfortable owning as a speculative core. It has a credible Phase 3-ready asset, another 2026 readout, early pipeline optionality, and runway into 2029.

DFTX is the name most likely to move fastest in either direction. That can be good if the data are clean, but painful if the Phase 3 readouts are mixed.

GHRS may still become a major winner if GH001 repeats its Phase 2b profile in pivotal work. I just do not think the current setup gives the same fresh upside as ATAI or the same near-term catalyst torque as DFTX.
