Context

This work sat within startup ecosystem and developer programme delivery across Asia-Pacific: supporting startup grant workflows, founder and participant engagement, university partnerships, learning campaigns, and public-facing ecosystem activations.

The practical thread was consistent: translate programme intent into coordination, follow-through, communications, milestone visibility, and partner delivery.

The experience covered TZ APAC / Tezos startup grant and ecosystem programmes, TezAsia, the NUS Computing x TZ APAC partnership, CoinGecko Learn & Earn, and Singapore FinTech Festival activation.

Operating Complexity

Ecosystem programme delivery required dependable coordination in an environment where stakeholder needs, programme stages, and public-facing activity could differ substantially.

  • Multiple stakeholders: founders and participants, education partners, developer communities, campaign partners, and event stakeholders required clear coordination.
  • Regional context: TezAsia and TZ APAC activity involved Asia-Pacific and multi-country ecosystem delivery.
  • Startup and Web3 ambiguity: founder, developer, grant, and community activities needed to be turned into workable programme steps.
  • Follow-through: communications, milestone tracking, partner coordination, and documentation supported reliable delivery.
