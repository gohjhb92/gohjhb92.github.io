Problem

Early-stage founders need specific support, not generic advice.

Early-stage ventures rarely need the same intervention at the same time. Some teams need customer discovery, some need product validation, some need market access, some need fundraising preparation, and others need help turning partner interest into a well-scoped pilot.

01

Support can be too generic

Founders may receive broad feedback even when the real blocker is more specific: weak evidence, unclear buyers, missing validation, or poor pilot definition.

02

Evidence is hard to see

Programme teams need a clear operating view of each startup's current blockers, proof points, mentor input, and next measurable action.

03

Introductions need timing

Mentors, partners, investors, and pilot opportunities should be matched when the startup has the right materials, readiness, and decision path.

A practical support operating system for venture-building teams.

VentureSupportOS gives programme teams a structured way to diagnose founder needs and assign support pathways across discovery, validation, market access, fundraising, pilot readiness, and mentor matching.

Fundraising Readiness Score

Business model15Revenue logic, buyer, pricing assumptions, and go-to-market approach.

Use of funds10Clear budget, runway logic, grant use, or investor capital allocation.
Milestone plan10Specific milestones tied to product, market, pilot, or revenue outcomes.
Investor fit10Alignment with investor, grant, accelerator, or institutional pathway.
80-100Ready for investor or grant review.
60-79Promising but needs stronger evidence.
40-59Needs more validation before fundraising.
Below 40Not fundraising-ready; return to discovery and validation.
