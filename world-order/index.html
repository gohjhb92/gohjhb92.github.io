Warsh Rate-Cut Thesis

A Productivity Wager, Not a Certainty

In this scenario, a Fed leadership persuaded that AI is lifting productivity may be more open to cutting rates as disinflation emerges. That case holds only if realized efficiency gains outrun the inflationary force of tariffs, energy constraints, wage pressure, fiscal expansion, and strategic buildout.

The risk is timing: AI may be disinflationary later, while the buildout is inflationary now.
