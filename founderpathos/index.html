Problem

Founders often move faster than their evidence.

Early-stage founders often jump too quickly from idea to product, fundraising, or partnerships without enough validation evidence. Incubators and startup support teams need a clearer way to diagnose stage, evidence gaps, and the right support pathway.

01

Idea quality is not validation

Founders can confuse a compelling idea with market proof, customer urgency, or willingness to pay.

02

Progress needs diagnosis

Programme teams need a structured way to understand what each founder has proven and what remains unclear.

03

Support changes by stage

Customer discovery, prototype development, product validation, pilots, funding, and commercialisation require different help.

04

Institutions need visibility

Teams need to know which startups are ready for grants, mentors, pilots, or investor introductions.

7-Stage Founder Journey

The path from idea to commercialisation.

Stage 1

Problem Discovery

Purpose
Prove the problem is real and worth solving.
Evidence
Problem statement, target user, workaround, pain severity, early interviews.
Support
Founder coaching, problem framing, discovery interview guide.
Stage 2

Customer Validation

Purpose
Prove the target customer cares.
Evidence
15-30 interviews, persona, buying trigger, pain level, willingness-to-pay signals.
Support
Discovery sprint, interview review, market segmentation.
Stage 3

Prototype

Purpose
Make the solution testable.
Evidence
Demo, wireframe, concept, model output, prototype walkthrough.
Support
Technical mentor, design review, MVP scoping.
Stage 4

MVP / Product Validation

Purpose
Test whether users can use it in a real workflow.
Evidence
MVP demo, user testing, workflow feedback, usage data, improvement metric.
Support
Product mentor, usability testing, feasibility review.
Stage 5

Pilot Readiness

Purpose
Prepare for a real-world partner test.
Evidence
Pilot objective, partner profile, metrics, data needs, risks, timeline.
Support
Pilot scoping, partner matching, risk review, metric design.
Stage 6

Funding Readiness

Purpose
Prepare for grants, angels, seed, institutional support, or strategic capital.
Evidence
Pitch deck, business model, traction, use of funds, roadmap, funding fit.
Support
Pitch review, grant readiness, investor matching, milestone planning.
Stage 7

Commercialisation

Purpose
Turn validation into customers, partnerships, revenue, investment, or venture formation.
Evidence
Pilot results, ROI case, pricing, pipeline, commercial follow-up plan.
Support
Market access, paid deployment planning, investor follow-up, partner conversion.

Pilot Readiness Checklist

Prepare before making the partner introduction.

Pilot Scope
  • Objective defined
  • Target partner identified
  • User group selected
  • Timeline drafted
Success Metrics
  • Business metric defined
  • Product metric defined
  • User feedback method defined
  • Post-pilot decision path defined
Risk Review
  • Data requirements known
  • Privacy considerations reviewed
  • Security assumptions documented
  • Compliance issues flagged
Implementation
  • Startup owner assigned
  • Partner owner assigned
  • Technical setup clarified
  • Support cadence defined
Funding Readiness

Score the evidence before grant or investor review.

Funding readiness means the startup has enough evidence, milestones, and clarity to justify grants, institutional support, angel funding, seed funding, or strategic investment.

CriteriaPointsWhat It Checks
Problem and market clarity15Clear problem, target user, urgency, and market scope.
Customer validation15Interview quality, pain severity, persona, and buying trigger evidence.
Product evidence15Prototype, MVP, demo, workflow fit, or technical feasibility.
Traction or pilot evidence15Users, pilots, partner interest, revenue, or measurable usage.
Team capability10Founder commitment, domain fit, technical capacity, and execution strength.
Business model10Revenue model, buyer, pricing assumptions, and go-to-market path.
Use-of-funds clarity10Clear capital use tied to validation, product, hiring, or market milestones.
Milestone roadmap10Specific next milestones and proof points for the next support round.
80-100Ready for grant or investor review.
60-79Promising but needs stronger evidence.
40-59Needs more validation.
Below 40Not funding-ready.

For Singapore founders, funding pathways may include Startup SG Founder, Startup SG Tech, Startup SG Equity, university venture programmes, institutional grants, angel investors, or seed investors depending on founder profile, stage, technology readiness, and evidence.
