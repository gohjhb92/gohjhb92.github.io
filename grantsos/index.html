Employer Case Study

Why this exists

GrantsOS exists to model a practical founder-support problem: founders need a clear way to determine which grant or institutional pathway fits their stage, eligibility, evidence quality, and next milestone.

Who it is for

Incubation managers, university venture teams, public-sector innovation teams, accelerator operators, grant support teams, and ecosystem partnership teams.

What it demonstrates

Founder intake design, grant readiness triage, eligibility mapping, scoring logic, founder evidence tracking, milestone checklists, and support route recommendations.

What is real vs demo

This is a self-directed portfolio prototype. Metrics, startup records, founder profiles, and scoring outputs shown are sample data unless otherwise stated.

How this relates to my target roles

The project demonstrates grant workflow thinking relevant to incubation, programme operations, founder support, university venture building, research commercialisation, and public-sector innovation teams.

Problem

Singapore founders need clearer grant pathway fit.

A first-time founder, university student team, research spin-off, deep-tech startup, and growth-stage startup may all need different forms of support, evidence, mentors, and financing routes.

01

Fragmented information

Grant information is spread across agencies, universities, incubators, and institutions.

02

Readiness confusion

Founders often mix up eligibility, application evidence, milestone readiness, and funding fit.

03

Triage takes time

Programme teams need a structured way to recommend grants, mentors, pilots, or investor pathways.

Solution

GrantsOS organises support routes into a structured explorer.

The dashboard turns founder profile, startup maturity, technology readiness, institution affiliation, and evidence gaps into a clear pathway recommendation workflow.

01

Grant Pathway Explorer

Compare founder, technology, institutional, financing, and ecosystem visibility pathways.

Pathway Map

Singapore Startup Grant Pathway Map

Each card summarises what the pathway is best suited for, what a programme team should track, and the signals that suggest a founder is ready for deeper review.

First-time founders

Startup SG Founder

Best for
First-time entrepreneurs with innovative business concepts.
Track
Founder eligibility, business concept, feasibility, management team, market potential, mentor recommendation, founder commitment.
Readiness signals
Clear problem, committed founding team, early validation, business model, mentor support.
Deep tech

Startup SG Tech

Best for
Startups commercialising innovative proprietary technology through proof-of-concept or proof-of-value projects.
Track
Technology novelty, IP status, prototype readiness, technical feasibility, POC/POV plan, commercial validation, project milestones.
Readiness signals
Technical differentiation, working prototype or research base, clear commercial use case, milestone plan.
Startup enablers

Startup SG Accelerator

Best for
Incubators, accelerators, and startup support organisations enabling access to markets, talent, funding, mentorship, and ecosystem support.
Track
Programme design, mentor network, startup outcomes, market access support, ecosystem impact, founder support model.
Readiness signals
Strong programme model, startup pipeline, mentor network, track record, ecosystem partnerships.
Investment

Startup SG Equity

Best for
High-potential technology startups preparing for qualified investor funding or co-investment pathways.
Track
Investor readiness, IP strength, traction, global market potential, fundraising pipeline, qualified investor interest.
Readiness signals
Scalable technology, investor materials, commercial traction, strong team, follow-on funding potential.
Debt financing

Startup SG Loan / Enterprise Financing

Best for
Startups and SMEs that need financing support for growth, working capital, fixed assets, trade, or expansion.
Track
Financial statements, repayment capacity, business model, growth plan, use of loan, risk profile.
Readiness signals
Revenue visibility, repayment ability, operating history, clear financing use case.
Ecosystem visibility

Startup SG Network

Best for
Startups seeking ecosystem visibility, partnership discovery, and connection to Singapore startup community resources.
Track
Company profile, sector, stage, partnership goals, ecosystem needs.
Readiness signals
Clear profile, market positioning, partnership objectives, active founder outreach.
University founders

NUS Venture Initiation Programme

Best for
NUS students or alumni pursuing innovative startup ideas.
Track
NUS affiliation, project scope, market need, prototype plan, 12-month milestone plan, budget use.
Readiness signals
Innovative idea, clear market need, NUS-affiliated founder, prototype plan, no major external funding conflict.
Research to venture

NUS GRIP / Research Commercialisation

Best for
NUS-linked researchers, postgraduate students, research staff, alumni, or technical teams translating research into deep-tech startups.
Track
Research origin, technical differentiation, business model design, market validation, mentor input, incubation milestones, investment readiness.
Readiness signals
Research-based technology, commercialisation potential, market validation, venture formation path.
National research

National GRIP / Research Institute Pathway

Best for
Researchers and innovators from Singapore universities and research institutes translating lab-based research into ventures.
Track
Research origin, technology readiness, IP status, commercial validation, mentor support, spin-off potential.
Readiness signals
Lab-to-market potential, technology defensibility, industry relevance, venture formation plan.
NTU support

NTUitive / NTU Innovation & Entrepreneurship

Best for
NTU-linked inventors, researchers, student founders, and startups seeking incubation, IP support, grant support, or venture-building guidance.
Track
Invention disclosure, IP readiness, proof-of-concept support, startup formation, mentor support, incubation status.
Readiness signals
NTU affiliation, invention or venture concept, technical development needs, commercialisation pathway.
SMU incubation

SMU IIE / BIG / Acceleration Grant

Best for
SMU-linked founders accepted into incubation and seeking growth support.
Track
BIG incubation status, mentor engagement, commercial potential, founder values, traction, milestone progress.
Readiness signals
Accepted into incubation, strong founder commitment, market opportunity, feasible business model, coachability.
SUTD support

SUTD Venture, Innovation & Entrepreneurship

Best for
SUTD students, alumni, faculty, and entrepreneurship programme teams seeking grants, prototype support, mentors, or venture investment.
Track
SUTD affiliation, prototype funding needs, mentor needs, design or AI innovation alignment, venture investment readiness.
Readiness signals
SUTD-linked team, product/prototype development need, venture potential, mentor or co-investment fit.
Polytechnic founders

Polytechnic Startup Support

Best for
Polytechnic student or alumni teams seeking early validation, mentorship, seed funding, incubation, or Startup SG Founder preparation.
Track
Institution affiliation, founder readiness, prototype progress, pitch readiness, funding need, mentor support.
Readiness signals
Student or alumni team, early idea or MVP, entrepreneurship centre engagement, clear next milestone.
