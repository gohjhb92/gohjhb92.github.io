Problem

Fintech and AI startup screening is difficult because product claims, market context, regulatory exposure, and readiness evidence do not arrive in a consistent form.

01

Uneven intake notes

Founder narratives and reviewer notes can differ in depth, making candidates difficult to compare.

02

Sector-specific risk

Payments, lending, wealth, insurance and compliance products raise different regulatory questions.

03

Unverified AI claims

Teams may describe automation as defensibility without clear data, controls, or operating evidence.

04

Readiness is multi-factor

Market, traction, compliance, product maturity and pilot conditions need structured review together.

05

Memos need consistency

Programme teams need repeatable briefs that record risks, questions and proposed support.

Singapore Relevance

The demo is framed around Singapore fintech evaluation, where an incubation team may need to ask how product claims meet regulatory, governance and pilot requirements in a MAS-regulated financial-services context.

AI Governance RegTech AML / Fraud / Scams SME Credit WealthTech Insurance Operations Payments Infrastructure

The point is not to claim regulatory approval. It is to show a review workflow that asks the right questions before recommending incubation support or a pilot conversation.

Output Examples

Sample outputs that turn screening observations into a usable follow-up record for a programme or innovation team.

Output 01

Startup screening memo

Problem, target customer, evidence summary, regulatory context, recommendation rationale and next action.

Output 02

Risk flags

Licensing assumptions, data governance, model oversight, fraud exposure and unsupported claims to verify.

Output 03

Pilot-readiness notes

Testing scope, prospective partner, data requirements, success criteria and operational blockers.

Output 04

Suggested incubation support

Mentor needs, regulatory guidance, partner access, validation tasks and programme checkpoints.

Output 05

Founder interview questions

Focused questions on traction evidence, AI claims, controls, compliance ownership and pilot conditions.

What This Proves For Employers

The project demonstrates practical thinking for teams screening fintech ventures and structuring the follow-up work that follows an initial evaluation.

Startup evaluation judgment

Turning varied founder information into a comparable review structure and clear next-step recommendation.

Sector research

Recognising that payments, SME finance, compliance, wealth and insurance require distinct questions.

Structured memo writing

Producing concise records of strengths, uncertainty, risk flags, diligence questions and support options.

Regulatory awareness

Framing regulatory exposure as an evaluation input without claiming compliance conclusions.

AI workflow design

Using assisted screening and drafting as support for accountable reviewer judgment, not a substitute for it.

Accelerator operations thinking

Connecting screening outcomes to founder interviews, mentorship, pilots and incubation support.
