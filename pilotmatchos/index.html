Problem

Pilot opportunities are hard to structure.

Many startups struggle to move from prototype or MVP into real-world validation because programme teams must coordinate startups, mentors, corporate partners, institutions, risk reviewers, and internal stakeholders before a pilot can launch.

01

Unclear partner needs

Startups are often not clear on what kind of pilot partner they need or what validation outcome they are seeking.

02

Partner confidence gaps

Corporate and institutional partners need clear pilot scope, risk areas, success metrics, and implementation timelines.

03

Weak operating visibility

Incubation teams need a structured way to track readiness, partner fit, introductions, outcomes, and follow-up actions.

Solution

PilotMatchOS turns pilot matching into an operating workflow.

The dashboard helps programme managers assess startup readiness, recommend suitable partner types, define pilot objectives, track risk areas, and monitor pilot outcomes.

01

Pilot Readiness Score

Score maturity, validation, partner fit, risk awareness, scope clarity, and implementation readiness.

02

Startup-to-Partner Match Matrix

Recommend partner categories based on startup needs, sector, use case, and evidence.

03

Pilot Scope Builder

Turn a startup opportunity into an objective, timeline, success metric, and owner-based pilot brief.

04

Risk & Compliance Checklist

Flag data, regulatory, technical, operational, and commercial risks before introductions.

05

Partner Introduction Tracker

Track warm introductions, partner owner, startup owner, review state, and next action.

06

Success Metrics Dashboard

Define adoption, accuracy, efficiency, ROI, user feedback, and integration measures.

07

Pilot Outcome Tracker

Monitor scoped, active, completed, paused, and follow-up pilot outcomes.

08

Follow-up Action Log

Capture commercial follow-ups, product refinements, partner reviews, and reporting actions.

Risk Review

Risk & Compliance Checklist

Data Risk

  • Does the pilot require sensitive data?
  • Can anonymised or synthetic data be used?
  • Are data access controls defined?

Regulatory Risk

  • Is the startup operating in a regulated sector?
  • Are licensing or compliance dependencies clear?
  • Is legal or compliance review needed?

Technical Risk

  • Does the solution require system integration?
  • Is the deployment environment defined?
  • Are uptime, security, or API requirements clear?

Operational Risk

  • Who owns the pilot on the partner side?
  • Is the workflow change manageable?
  • Are users available for testing?

Commercial Risk

  • Is there a clear buyer?
  • Is there a budget owner?
  • What happens after a successful pilot?
