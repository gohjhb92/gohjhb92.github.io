00bMissions

Take and HoldObjective control across the battlefield▸
Battlefield Dominancevs T&H
Score VP at the end of each battle round for each objective marker you control. The player who controls more objectives scores a bonus. Max 45VP.
Immovable Objectvs PTF
Score VP for objectives controlled, with a bonus for holding your Home objective. Rewards a defensive anchor while pushing out to contest.
Determined Acquisitionvs Disruption
Score VP for controlling objectives across the battlefield, with emphasis on objectives in No Man's Land and enemy territory.
Purge and Securevs Recon
Score VP for controlling objectives each round. Combines holding ground with pressure to deny your opponent's positioning.
Inescapable Dominionvs PA
Score VP for controlling objectives across all zones. Rewards spreading control and denying the opponent their operation markers.
Purge the FoeDestroy enemies and contest objectives▸
Unstoppable Forcevs T&H
Score VP for destroying enemy units and controlling objectives. Rewards aggression — advance and eliminate to score.
Meatgrindervs PTF
Score VP primarily for destroying enemy units. Both players are rewarded for combat — the most destructive force wins.
Punishmentvs Disruption
Score VP for destroying enemy units, with bonus scoring for eliminating units in specific zones. Make the enemy pay for disrupting your lines.
Consecratevs Recon
Score VP for destroying enemies and holding objectives. Combines combat attrition with area control to deny the opponent's surveilling.
Destroyer's Wrathvs PA
Score VP for destroying enemy units, especially units holding or near objectives. Punish the opponent for securing their priority assets.
DisruptionTactical placement, traps, and area denial▸
Death Trapvs T&H
Place trap markers on terrain areas. Score VP when enemy units are destroyed while within a trapped terrain area.
Delaying Actionvs PTF
Score VP for surviving and contesting. Reward yourself for keeping units alive in contested zones and slowing the enemy's advance.
Outmanoeuvrevs Disruption
Score VP for controlling objectives in the enemy's territory. Both players manoeuvre to seize ground beyond the centreline.
Smoke and Mirrorsvs Recon
Score VP through tactical repositioning and deception. Reward fast-moving or hidden units that exploit gaps in the enemy's survey.
Locate and Denyvs PA
Score VP by placing operation markers on objectives and denying the opponent's markers. Control placement to shut down Priority Assets scoring.
ReconnaissanceSurveil, sweep zones, and gather intelligence▸
Reconnaissance Sweepvs T&H
Score VP for having units present in different battlefield zones. Rewards a mobile, spread-out force that denies your opponent easy objective control.
Triangulationvs PTF
Score VP for controlling a triangle of key objectives. Map out and hold the right combination to maximise your VP against a destructive opponent.
Surveil the Foevs Disruption
Place operation markers on objectives. Score VP for surveilling (observing) enemy units — surveilling is blocked if the unit is near an objective that has your marker.
Gather Intelvs Recon
Score VP for surveilling enemy units and controlling objectives. Both sides race to gather the most intelligence across the battlefield.
Search and Scourvs PA
Score VP for sweeping the battlefield — having units in each quarter of the board and controlling objectives, especially in enemy territory.
Priority AssetsOperation markers, relics, and special objectives▸
Secure Assetvs T&H
Place operation markers on objectives at the start. Score VP for controlling objectives that have your operation markers on them.
Vital Linkvs PTF
Place operation markers and maintain a chain of control including the Central objective. Score VP for the chain; score bonus VP for markers on the central objective.
Extract Relicvs Disruption
A relic marker is placed on the central objective. Score VP for controlling it and for moving it toward your battlefield edge each round.
Vanguard Operationvs Recon
Score VP for advancing units forward — units in No Man's Land and the opponent's territory score more. Rewards aggressive deployment and positioning.
Sabotagevs PA
Place sabotage markers on objectives. Score VP for controlling objectives with your markers and for sabotaging the opponent's objectives.
Note: Mission descriptions above are thematic summaries. Full scoring rules are on your Primary Mission card from the Chapter Approved Mission Deck.

01Core Concepts

Coherency & EngagementThe two distances that govern everything▸
Coherency2" / 9"
Every model must be within 2" (horizontal, 5" vertical) of at least one other model in its unit, and within 9" (5" vertical) of every other model in the unit. Checked on set-up and at the end of any move. Out of coherency at end of turn = forced model removal until fixed (they count as destroyed, no death triggers).
Engagement range2" / 5"
A model's engagement range is 2" horizontally / 5" vertically around it. Any friendly model within an enemy's engagement range makes both units engaged. No engaged models in a unit = that unit is unengaged.
Always measure to/from the closest point of a model's base (or its closest point if it's a basesless FRAME model — common on big Monsters/Vehicles like Knights).
Dice, Leadership & Battle-shock2D6 rolls and what failing them does▸

Leadership roll: roll 2D6 — succeeds if the result ≥ the unit's Ld characteristic.

Battle-shock roll = a Leadership roll. Fail it and every model in the unit becomes battle-shocked: OC drops to '-', the unit can't be targeted by your own stratagems, and it can't start (or finish) actions.

Dice shorthand: 2+/3+ = that result or higher · 1-3 = any result in that range · 2D6 = roll & add · D3 = 1D6 halved, rounded up · D6+1 = roll then add the flat number.

VisibilityVisible vs Fully Visible▸

Line of sight = an unobstructed straight line from any part of the observer to any part of the target, ignoring other models in either unit.

Visible: any part of the model can be seen. Fully visible: every part facing the observer can be seen (only that model itself may block its own parts). A unit is visible if 1+ model in it is visible; fully visible only if every model in it is fully visible.

02The Battle Round

Turn structureStart → 5 phases → End, then opponent's turn▸

Each battle round: both players take one full turn (Start of Turn → Command → Movement → Shooting → Charge → Fight → End of Turn). The same player always goes first in every round of a given mission.

Command PhaseGain CP, then check morale▸

1. Both players gain +1 CP (Core CP) — every turn, not just your own.

2. Battle-shock check: the active player must make a battle-shock roll for every one of their units that is already battle-shocked or at/below half-strength (by models, or by remaining wounds for a 1-model Vehicle/Monster unit).

3. Resolve any Command-phase abilities (auras, faction rules, etc.).

Movement PhaseMove, Advance, Fall Back, or Remain Stationary▸

Each unit picks one: Remain Stationary · Normal Move (up to M", must end unengaged) · Advance Move (M + 1D6", can't charge or start an action this turn) · Fall Back (must be Engaged — Ordered Retreat if unshocked, Desperate Escape if shocked or chosen).

Disembarking from Transports also happens here (Rapid / Tactical / Combat — see Movement Phase section). Units arriving from Reserves make an Ingress move this phase.

Shooting TypesNormal / Assault / Close-Quarters / Indirect▸
Normal Shooting
Unengaged, didn't Advance this turn. Standard shoot — use Making Attacks.
Assault Shooting
Unengaged and Advanced this turn, has a [ASSAULT] weapon. Can only fire [ASSAULT] weapons this phase.
Close-Quarters Shooting
Engaged, didn't Advance, has a [CLOSE-QUARTERS] weapon or is a Monster/Vehicle. Can target units you're engaged with. Monster/Vehicle models take -1 to hit unless firing a [CLOSE-QUARTERS] weapon at an engaged target; [BLAST] still can't target engaged units.
Indirect Shooting
Unengaged, didn't Advance, has an [INDIRECT FIRE] weapon. Can target units it can't see. Target always gets cover; no hit re-rolls; unmodified 1-5 always fails (1-3 only if you stayed stationary and a friendly unit can see the target).
Charge Phase2D6 charge roll, 12" declare range▸

Declare a charge against one or more enemy units within 12". Roll 2D6 for the max charge distance. To succeed, every model must end up engaged with all declared targets and engaged with nothing else. Can't declare if engaged, if you Advanced/Fell Back this turn, or if no enemy is within 12".

A successful charge grants Fights First for the rest of the turn — and remember, a roll of 2 can never complete a charge (you can't end within engagement range starting outside it on a roll that low).

Fight PhasePile in → Fight → Consolidate▸

1. Pile In (3") — every engaged unit (or one that charged, or made an overrun fight) can move 3" closer to its engaged/charge targets. Models already in base contact can't move.

2. Fight — Fights First units go first (players alternate picking one each), then everyone else (players alternate again). A unit fights if it's engaged, was engaged at the start of the step, or charged this turn. An Overrun fight (unengaged units that were engaged at step-start, or just became engaged) gets an extra pile-in move before fighting — this can drag in fresh enemy units, who then become eligible to fight too.

3. Consolidate (3") — pick a mode: Ongoing (still engaged — close the gap with someone you're engaged with), Engaging (within 3" of an enemy not yet engaged — move to engage; can pull in enemies who haven't fought yet), or Objective (within 3" of an objective and no enemy nearby — move onto it).

06Objectives

Control & SecuredIt's just OC math▸

Sum the OC characteristic of every model you have within range of an objective (within its terrain area). Highest total controls it; a tie means nobody controls it unless it's already secured.

Secured: some rules let your army keep control of an objective even after you leave it — until the opponent's level of control becomes higher than yours at the end of a phase.

Battle-shocked models count their OC as '-' (zero, can't help control anything).

07Actions

Starting & completingMission/codex actions follow this pattern▸

A unit can't start an action if it's off the battlefield, an Aircraft/Fortification, battle-shocked, has OC 0 or '-', engaged (unless Titanic), or already Advanced/Fell Back/started another action this turn.

Once started, that unit can't shoot (unless Titanic) or charge for the rest of the turn. If it makes any move other than pile-in/consolidate, or leaves the battlefield, the action fails to complete — no effect.

08Hidden & Gone to Ground

HiddenInvisible beyond detection range▸
How to be Hidden
A model is hidden while all of the following apply:
  • Has the INFANTRY / BEASTS / SWARM keyword
  • Is within a terrain area containing one or more light or dense terrain features
  • Its unit did not make any ranged attacks this turn or the previous turn
Effect — Detection Range15" default
A hidden model can only be visible to enemy models within its detection range (15" unless otherwise stated). Outside that range, it counts as not visible regardless of actual line of sight.

Note: detection range cannot be modified to better than 9" or worse than 30". Some models (e.g. TOWERING) cannot benefit from Hidden at all.
Hidden applies per model, not per unit. If any non-hidden model in the unit is visible and in range, Hidden offers no protection — the unit can still be targeted through that model.
Gone to Ground–3" to detection range behind solid terrain▸
How to have Gone to Ground
A model has gone to ground while all of the following apply:
  • It is Hidden
  • It is not fully visible to the attacking model due to one or more intervening solid terrain features
  • Its unit did not make any ranged attacks this turn or the previous turn
Effect−3" detection range
Subtract 3" from that model's detection range. At the default 15", this reduces detection range to 12" against that attacking model.

Unlike Hidden, Gone to Ground is not a fixed state — it depends on the specific attacking model's perspective and which solid terrain features intervene. A model may have it against one attacker but not another.
Light terrain only? Light terrain features are not Solid, so they cannot grant Gone to Ground — even though they do allow Hidden. Gone to Ground only applies when Solid terrain blocks the attacker's line of sight.

Resolve failed saves from lowest roll to highest. Selected model loses W equal to the weapon's D. 0 or fewer wounds remaining = destroyed. Once a whole allocation group is dead, move to the next group in order.

Making Attacks — the 3 steps before all thatSelect weapons → targets → resolve▸

1. Select Weapons — shooting: pick any number of ranged weapons. Fighting: pick exactly one melee weapon per model.

2. Select Targets — shooting: one target per weapon, must be visible, in range, unengaged. Fighting: one or more engaged enemy units; melee weapons targeting 2+ units must split their attacks across them (declare how many attacks go where).

3. Resolve Attacks — pick an enemy unit, gather attack dice from one weapon (plus any other weapons making identical attacks against the same unit), resolve the full Hit→Wound→Save→Damage sequence, repeat until every weapon's attacks are resolved.

Identical attacks share BS/WS, S, AP and D and the same abilities — bundle their dice together to save time.
Mortal Wounds & Hazard RollsBypass the normal sequence entirely▸

Mortal wounds skip straight to damage, one at a time: hit a wounded non-Character model first if one exists, else any non-Character model, else a wounded Character, else any Character. 1 wound lost per mortal wound.

If an attack causes both normal damage and mortal wounds, resolve all the normal damage first, then the mortal wounds.

Hazard roll (Desperate Escape, [HAZARDOUS] weapons, overheating, etc.): roll 1D6 per model — on a 1-2, the unit suffers 1 mortal wound (3 instead if every model in the unit is Monster/Vehicle).

05Command Phase

Command Phase sequenceCP gain → Battle-shock → abilities▸
1. Gain CP
Both players gain +1 Command Point (Core CP) — every turn, not just your own. This happens simultaneously at the start of the Command phase.
2. Battle-shock Checks
The active player must make a Battle-shock roll for every friendly unit that is already Battle-shocked OR at or below half-strength. Half-strength = half the starting number of models (or half starting wounds for a single-model Vehicle/Monster unit).
3. Command Phase Abilities
Resolve any abilities that trigger in the Command phase — auras, faction rules, detachment abilities, enhancements, etc. Active player resolves theirs first, then the opponent resolves theirs.
Battle-shock effectsWhat it means to be Battle-shocked▸
Battle-shocked unit
  • OC drops to '-' (counts as 0 — cannot help control objectives)
  • Cannot be targeted by your own stratagems
  • Cannot start or complete actions
  • On a Fall Back, must use Desperate Escape (hazard roll per model)
Recovering
At the start of your next Command phase, make another Battle-shock roll — succeed and the unit recovers. The Insane Bravery stratagem (1CP) auto-passes one roll before it's made, once per battle.

11Shooting Phase

Eligibility & Target SelectionWho can shoot and what they can shoot at▸
Eligible to Shoot
A unit can shoot if it is on the battlefield and has not Fallen Back this turn. Engaged units can only shoot if they have a [CLOSE-QUARTERS] weapon or are a Monster/Vehicle (Close-Quarters Shooting). Units that Advanced can only fire [ASSAULT] weapons.
Select Targets
Each ranged weapon targets one enemy unit. The target must be visible to the firing model and within range of the weapon. Engaged enemy units cannot be targeted unless firing via Close-Quarters Shooting. [BLAST] weapons can never target engaged units.
Snap Shooting
Some effects (e.g. Fire Overwatch stratagem) force Snap Shooting: hits only on an unmodified 6, no re-rolls to hit allowed. Other modifiers to the hit roll still apply after the 6 threshold is met.
Shooting TypesNormal / Assault / Close-Quarters / Indirect▸
Normal Shooting
Unit is unengaged and did not Advance this turn. Pick any ranged weapons — use Making Attacks sequence.
Assault Shooting
Unit is unengaged and did Advance this turn. Can only fire [ASSAULT] weapons. No other ranged weapons allowed this phase.
Close-Quarters Shooting
Unit is Engaged, did not Advance, has a [CLOSE-QUARTERS] weapon or is a Monster/Vehicle. Can only target units it is Engaged with. Monster/Vehicle models take −1 to hit unless the weapon is [CLOSE-QUARTERS]. [BLAST] weapons still cannot target engaged units.
Indirect Fire
Unit is unengaged and did not Advance. Has an [INDIRECT FIRE] weapon. Can target units it cannot see. Target always gets Benefit of Cover. No re-rolls to hit. Unmodified hit rolls of 1–5 always fail (reduced to 1–3 only if the firer Remained Stationary and a friendly unit has line of sight to the target).

12Charge / Fight Phase

Charge PhaseRoll → Pick Targets → Move▸
Declare a Charge2D6" range
Roll 2D6 first — this is your max charge distance. Then pick one or more enemy units within that distance as targets. You cannot attempt a charge if your unit is Engaged, has Advanced or Fallen Back this turn. You may choose not to execute the move after rolling.
Charge Moveup to 2D6"
Move each model up to the rolled distance. Each model must end closer to a charge target, within 1" of a target if possible (or at least within Engagement Range if 1" is not achievable). Cannot end Engaged with any unit not picked as a target. A roll of 2 auto-fails. Successful charge grants FIGHTS FIRST for the rest of the turn.
Heroic InterventionStratagem
End of opponent's Charge phase: unengaged unit within 12" of an enemy makes a charge (1CP). Leap to Defend — must target units that already charged this phase. Into the Fray (+1CP) — can target any enemy units within 6", but charge roll is capped at 6.
Fight Phase — Order of PlayFights First → everyone else, players alternate▸
Step 1 — Pile In3"
Active Player moves all eligible units first, then Opposing Player. Each eligible unit (Engaged, or charged this turn) may move each model up to 3". If already Engaged, you must pick all currently Engaged units as targets; if not yet Engaged, pick one or more units within 5". Each model must end closer to and (if possible) Engaged with the nearest selected target. Models already in base contact cannot move.
Step 2 — Fight
Fights First units go first — active player picks one, then opponent picks one, alternating until all Fights First units have fought. Then all remaining eligible units fight the same way (alternate). A unit is eligible if it is Engaged, was Engaged at the start of this step, or charged this turn.
Overrun Fight
If a unit was Engaged at the start of the Fight step but its enemies were all destroyed before its turn to fight — it gets an extra Pile In move (3"), which can pull in new enemy units. Those newly-engaged enemies then become eligible to fight in the current step.
Step 3 — Consolidate3"
Active Player consolidates all units first, then Opposing Player. Each unit eligible to fight this phase consolidates using one mode: Ongoing — currently Engaged, move up to 3" closer to an Engaged enemy. Engaging — not Engaged but within 3" of an enemy, move to Engage (units pulled in become eligible to fight via Overrun Fight). Objective — neither above applies, but within 3" of an objective; move up to 3" within or toward it.
Fight Phase — Key RestrictionsWhat you can and can't do▸
Fights First
Granted by a successful Charge, or by certain abilities (e.g. FIGHTS FIRST keyword). Both players' Fights First units resolve before any others. If only one side has Fights First units left, the active player picks one of theirs, then the opponent picks one, and so on.
Weapon Selection (Melee)
Pick exactly one melee weapon per model. [EXTRA ATTACKS] weapons fight alongside one other chosen weapon. If attacking 2+ enemy units, declare how many of the model's attacks go at each target before rolling.
Fallen Back / Advanced
A unit that Fell Back or Advanced this turn cannot fight in the Fight phase.
Counteroffensive2CP
Opponent's Fight phase, right after an enemy unit resolves attacks: your eligible unit gains FIGHTS FIRST and must be the very next unit you pick to fight.

13Emergency Disembark

Transport destroyed while carrying passengersTriggered immediately when the transport is destroyed▸
Emergency Disembark6"
Transport just destroyed. Each model makes a Hazard Roll first; models that can't fit a legal position within 6" are destroyed. Unit ends Battle-shocked and cannot Charge this turn.

14Terrain & Cover

Terrain categories & movementExposed / Light / Dense▸

Exposed/Light — every model moves through freely, horizontally and vertically.

Dense — Infantry/Beasts/Swarm/Mobile move through it horizontally freely; Infantry/Beasts/Swarm also vertically. Everything else can only pass horizontally through sections 2" or less tall (otherwise must climb up/over) and can't pass through floors/ceilings.

Big Vehicles/Monsters without Mobile generally have to go around dense terrain over 2" tall, or climb it section by section — plan large model movement around table clutter.

Cover, Hidden, Obscuring, SolidThe four visibility rules that matter most▸

Benefit of Cover (-1 to the attack's BS): applies if every model in the target unit is either (a) Infantry/Beasts/Swarm and inside a terrain area, or (b) not fully visible to the shooter because of intervening terrain.

Hidden: Infantry/Beasts/Swarm models inside a terrain area containing one or more light or dense terrain features, whose unit hasn't shot this turn or last turn, are only visible within 15" (their "detection range") unless stated otherwise. (Rules Commentary update — printed rules say dense only.)

Obscuring: a terrain area with light or dense features blocks visibility entirely between any two models whose line of sight crosses it (unless one of them is inside that same area).

Solid: dense terrain blocks line of sight through any enclosed gap (doors, windows) that's 3" or less off the ground — no peeking through ground-floor windows.

STEALTH and [IGNORES COVER] directly fight over this -1 BS — see Glossary.

15Vehicles, Transports & Advanced Rules

Monsters & VehiclesBig models play by different rules▸

On a Normal/Advance move, your Monster/Vehicle models can move through any model except other Monster/Vehicle models.

FRAME models (most baseless Monsters/Vehicles) measure from their closest point rather than a base, and rotate around their central axis.

Shooting an engaged Monster/Vehicle: it can be targeted by ranged attacks even while engaged — but subtract 1 from the hit roll, unless the attack uses [CLOSE-QUARTERS] from a unit it's engaged with.

TransportsEmbark, ride, disembark▸

Embark: after a normal/advance/fall-back move, within 3" of a friendly Transport with room, unit wasn't set up this turn → it's removed from the table, "inside" the Transport.

Disembark (your Movement phase) — three modes, checked in order:

  • Rapid — transport moved normally or Ingressed. Set up within 3". No Charge this turn. Inherits any Ingress set-up restrictions.
  • Tactical — before transport moves, or it Remained Stationary. Set up within 3", then immediately make a Normal or Advance Move (transport stays in place).
  • Combat — only if 3" set-up is blocked (usually Engagement Range). Set up within 6"; may be in Engagement Range of enemies Engaged with the transport. Whole unit makes Hazard Rolls + Battle-shock test.

Large bases (>3"): use 1" instead of 3" for Rapid & Tactical. Transport destroyed: Emergency Disembark — hazard roll, 6", unit Battle-shocked, can't Charge.

Firing Deck X lets up to X embarked models each fire one weapon through the Transport when it shoots — but then those models' own units can't shoot this turn.
Attached Units (Leader / Support)Characters joining a squad▸

A LEADER or SUPPORT unit can join one eligible bodyguard unit before the battle, forming a single attached unit for all rules purposes.

Targeting it: use the highest Toughness among the bodyguard models (or among the leader/support models if no bodyguards remain) for the whole attack — even if a different model in the unit gets the actual wounds.

Keywords: the attached unit has every keyword from every component unit — but individual models don't gain keywords they didn't already have.

Ability duration: a buff from the leader lasts only until the last leader model is destroyed; a buff from the bodyguard's own ability lasts until the last bodyguard model dies; wargear/enhancement abilities die with their specific bearer.

Strategic Reserves & Deep StrikeHolding units back, then dropping them in▸

Up to 50% of your points can sit in strategic reserves before the battle (including anything embarked in a reserved Transport). They arrive via an Ingress move from round 2 onward — 6" from a battlefield edge, more than 8" from every enemy, and not in your opponent's zone before round 3 (unless a rule overrides this, like DEEP STRIKE, which skips the edge and zone restrictions entirely).

Anything still in reserves after round 3 ends is destroyed — except units embarked in a Transport that itself already arrived, or "repositioned" units that left the table mid-battle via a specific rule.

Flying & SurgingFLY keyword interactions▸

A FLY unit can declare "taking to the skies" on a normal/advance/fall-back/charge move: -2" max distance (unless it also has HOVER, which cancels that penalty), but it can then ignore vertical distance entirely and pass through any model or terrain, including enemies and other Vehicles/Monsters.

Other Rules — Aura, Faction, Psychic, Wargear, Plunging FireAuras, factions, psychic, wargear, height bonuses▸

Aura abilities affect everything within range, including the bearer's own unit; stacking the same aura from two sources on one unit doesn't double it.

Faction abilities only work if your chosen army faction matches a faction keyword on that datasheet.

Psychic abilities — wounds they cause count as from a "psychic attack" for other rules that care.

Wargear abilities apply to the whole unit if the unit has the item, or just to the carrying model ("the bearer") if it's a personal item — until that bearer dies.

Plunging Fire (+1 BS): shooting a ground-level target from 3"+ up a terrain feature, or from a TOWERING model within 12" of the target.

AircraftRare for ground-heavy lists, included for completeness▸

Must start in strategic reserves; can only ever arrive via Ingress moves and get pulled back into reserves at the end of every opponent's turn. Can't be charged except by Fly units, and can't charge at all except to engage Fly units. Plunging Fire never applies to or from them.
